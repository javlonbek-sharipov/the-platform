How the-platform is designed, in one place:

- its **glossaries**, which say what each context's words mean;
- its **ADRs**, which record the decisions that are hard to reverse, and why each was made;
- its **specs**, which say what is being built;
- its **Diagrams**, which show how its parts talk to each other.

Every other page is a file of the repo, shown from where it lives. To change a page, edit its file.

## Exploring a Diagram

A Diagram on a page is more than a picture:

- drag it to move around it;
- click into it, then turn the mouse wheel to zoom, or pinch it on a trackpad;
- press `+` and `-` to zoom, and `0` to fit it to its frame again.
